Privacy policy

Last updated 16 Oct 2026

This policy covers anandtrainsyou.in (ATcoaching) and its private posting studio, a tool for publishing ATcoaching’s own videos to ATcoaching’s own social media accounts.

The public website

You can read the articles and guides and download plans without an account. We don’t ask for your name or email on this site. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure.

Coaching applications go through a Tally form, and store purchases go through the checkout provider named at checkout. Their own privacy policies apply to anything you enter there.

The posting studio

The studio at anandtrainsyou.in/dashboard is private. Only the site owner can sign in. It connects to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn so the owner can upload and schedule videos to the owner’s own accounts. It does not collect data about other people.

When the owner connects an account, the studio receives and stores:

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens that let it post on that account’s behalf
  • Basic profile details needed to post: account ID, display name and, for TikTok, the posting options TikTok allows for the account
  • The videos, cover images, captions and schedules the owner creates, plus the IDs and links of published posts

This data is used only to publish and schedule the owner’s posts and show their status. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

Data is stored on Cloudflare (D1 database and R2 storage). Tokens are encrypted before they are stored and are never sent to the browser. The studio and its API sit behind Cloudflare Access sign-in.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected, then deleted. Videos and drafts are kept until the owner deletes them. You can also revoke the studio’s access at any time from each platform:

  • Google / YouTube: Google security settings
  • Instagram and Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services

YouTube and Google

The studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. ATcoaching’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
