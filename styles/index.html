Three directions for the ATcoaching site

Each option is the same homepage with the same content, styled three ways. All three use blue. Pick the one that feels most like you, and the rest of the site (articles, free guides, store) gets built in that style.

Text, prices and products are placeholders for now. The grey boxes are where your photos go.
