Every few weeks someone asks for "the best exercise to lose belly fat". The honest answer: there isn't one.

## The myth

Spot reduction is the idea that training a muscle burns the fat sitting on top of it. Do enough crunches and your stomach gets flatter. Do enough tricep kickbacks and the back of your arms tighten up.

It sounds logical. It just doesn't hold up. Studies that have people train one area hard for weeks keep finding the same thing: fat loss happens across the whole body, not where you're working.

## What actually happens

When you're in a calorie deficit, your body pulls stored fat from all over. *Where* it comes from first is mostly decided by genetics, sex and hormones, not by which exercise you did today. For a lot of people, the stomach and lower back are the last places to lean out. Annoying, but normal.

> Your abs are already there. They're just under a layer you remove with diet, not with crunches.

## What to do instead

- **Run a moderate calorie deficit** and keep it going for months, not weeks.
- **Lift the whole body**, so you keep muscle while you lose fat.
- **Train abs like any other muscle**: a few hard sets, a couple of times a week, for strength and shape, not as a fat-burner.
- **Be patient with the last bit.** The stubborn areas go last. That's when most people quit, right before it works.

Crunches are fine. They're just not a fat-loss tool.
