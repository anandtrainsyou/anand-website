The approach

What actually matters

Progressive overload

Do a little more over time: more weight, more reps, or better form. This is the engine of every good program.

Consistency over intensity

A decent plan you follow for six months beats a perfect plan you quit after three weeks.

Recovery is training

Sleep, protein, and rest days are when you actually grow. Don't skip them.

Workout splits

Pick a split that fits your week

A split is just how you divide your training across the week. The best one is the one you can stick to.

2-3 daysBeginner

Full Body

Train everything each session. High frequency, great for learning the lifts and for busy schedules.

4 daysIntermediate

Upper / Lower

Alternate upper and lower body days. Each muscle gets hit twice a week with plenty of recovery.

3-6 daysInt. / Advanced

Push / Pull / Legs

Group muscles by movement. Run it once a week for 3 days, or twice for a high-volume 6-day split.

5 daysAdvanced

Body Part ("Bro") Split

One or two muscle groups per day. Lots of volume per session, but each muscle trained only once a week.
